# Belief register

**What this is for.** Before you look at the evidence, write down what you already
believe. The QA pass checks your findings against this list, so it can tell a finding
that came from participants apart from one that came from you, your boss, or a
report you read once.

**How to fill it in**
- **Write your own.** Each person writes their own beliefs before discussing them
  with anyone. Nobody — including this skill — writes them for you. The skill may ask
  questions that help you get started; it won't suggest answers.
- **One sentence per belief.** If you need "and", it's probably two beliefs.
- **About:** `people` is how users live, act or feel. `product` is the thing you make
  or the experience it gives.
- **Confidence:** `high` or `low`. There's no medium. "Pretty sure" counts as low.
- **Source type:** where the belief really comes from, not where you'd like it to.
  `own_experience` (you've lived it), `proxy` (someone you know: a partner, a parent),
  `authority` (a founder's, executive's, expert's or influential colleague's opinion),
  `internal_research`, `third_party_study`, `analytics`, `media`.
- **Source ref:** a link or the name of the study or report, if there is one.
- **Agreed by all:** `yes` only if **everyone** agrees. One person disagreeing makes it
  `no`, and the belief is an open question, not a working assumption. Say who
  disagrees and why in the dissent note.

Leave a field blank rather than guess. The QA pass will tell you which checks it
couldn't run because of it.
